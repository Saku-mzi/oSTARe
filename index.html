what is oSTARe?

A limited-run fragrance line rooted in street culture, built around individuality, creativity, and the confidence to move differently. We believe fragrance should do more than smell good. It should say something before you do.
Every oSTARe scent is created with presence: bold, expressive, and designed to leave an impression. From the fragrance itself to the bottle it comes in, every detail carries its own attitude. Our releases are intentionally small. Every two months, we introduce a limited drop of around 10 bottles per fragrance. No overproduction. No endless restocks. Just a small number of bottles for people who connect with the scent and what it represents. oSTARe makes distinctive fragrance more accessible without making it ordinary. It is for those who choose their own style, create their own lane, and do not need approval to stand out.
